Featured project

Bilingual Verse Interleaver Prototype

Many Bible sites help people read Chinese and English side by side. This tool solves a different problem: preparing materials. You paste your own Chinese and English text, choose which language comes first, and get verse-by-verse pairs that copy cleanly into a handout, slide or message, with a warning if a verse is missing, duplicated or does not match.

It is built so ministers can cut the copy-and-paste work and spend more time with people, without lowering the quality of their materials.

Works today:

  • Verse pairing by verse number or chapter:verse
  • English-first or Chinese-first order
  • Warnings for missing, duplicate or mismatched verses
  • Copy and print

Not built yet: PPTX export, source and copyright notices, and grouping for general bilingual documents.

The code is currently private and is available on request. I built it with AI assistance (Claude and Perplexity), starting from a real workflow problem.

Serving Christian ministries

I volunteer with an evangelical outreach ministry in Hong Kong, supporting teams that serve in both English and Chinese.

During outreaches and church events, the time that matters most is spent with people. That leaves little room to prepare or change materials, especially bilingual ones, where an edit in one language means updating the other and keeping the layout intact.

I build tools to take that pressure off, so ministers can stay on top of their work without losing time with the congregation. Good materials should not cost ministry time, and being a pastor should not mean accepting lower-quality work.
